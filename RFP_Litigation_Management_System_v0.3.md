### Background and Summary of Solution requirement

The proposed solution is an integrated Legal, Litigation and Recovery Management System designed to manage the complete legal lifecycle from pre-litigation activities through litigation, auction, recovery and settlement. It will integrate with the Core Banking System (CBS) to provide accurate account-wise financial information, while also managing legal documents, hearing dates, statutory deadlines, warrants, legal costs, opinions, communications and assigned tasks. The solution is required to reduce manual processes and errors, ensure timely action on critical legal deadlines, improve accountability and transparency, and provide a centralized 360-degree view of each account and case. It will also provide management dashboards, reports, alerts and audit trails to support effective decision-making, regulatory compliance and overall legal and recovery performance.

The selected solution provider will be responsible for implementing a centralized Litigation Management System for the Bank's Legal Department with the following key abilities:

### a. Data Integration and Source Connectivity

The system must be capable of seamless and secure integration with the Bank's existing and future systems required for legal, litigation, recovery, and reporting activities, including:

- **Core Banking Systems (CBS):** Integration with the Bank's relevant conventional, Islamic, offshore, and other CBS platforms/databases to retrieve account-wise customer and financial information, including outstanding balances, interest, unapplied interest, repayment history and classification status.
- **Recovery and Account Information:** Access to required account, recovery and financial information to support litigation, execution, settlement and recovery activities.
- **Document and Enterprise Systems:** Integration with relevant document management, workflow and enterprise systems where required for retrieval and archival of legal documents and records.
- **Regulatory and Reporting Interfaces:** Integration with relevant regulatory systems and data sources required for legal and compliance reporting.
- **Finance Systems:** Integration with Finance-related systems for processing and reconciliation of lawyer fees, court fees and other legal costs.
- **Enterprise Data Warehouse / MIS:** Integration with EDW, MIS and other reporting platforms where required for management dashboards, analytics and regulatory reporting.
- **Third-Party Systems and APIs:** Capability to integrate with approved third-party systems, APIs and middleware required to exchange legal, customer, account, recovery or other relevant information.
- **Real-Time and Batch Data Integration:** Support for real-time and batch data exchange using APIs, web services, message queues, ETL processes, database connectivity, or file-based interfaces as appropriate.
- **Bi-Directional Integration:** Where required, support secure bi-directional integration with CBS and other relevant Bank systems to ensure timely synchronization of information and transaction/status updates.

The integration framework shall ensure data accuracy, consistency, security, traceability and availability across the Litigation Management System and the Bank's relevant source systems, while minimizing manual data entry and duplication.
